Our story

Essor Automations was founded in April 2026 and builds automation software for small and medium businesses. We started with custom development work, and as the same problems kept coming up with different clients, we turned those solutions into proper products — so the next business would not have to pay to build the same thing from scratch. We have served 32 businesses so far.

Today we have seven products. ChatXFlow, DawaiStore, PanelSuite, Autolyst and Admetics are live, while Essor CRM and PRO ERP are in development and launching soon.

Alongside the software we run a marketing and ads team — Meta and Google campaigns, landing pages, WhatsApp automation and SEO. It exists because we kept watching good software sit unused while the business had no reliable way to bring customers to it.

If something in your business is being done by hand every day and could be done automatically, there is a reasonable chance we have already built the thing that does it.
